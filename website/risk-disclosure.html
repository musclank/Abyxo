RISK DISCLOSURE

Digital assets and meme coins can involve extreme and total-loss risks.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Read this before interacting with any digital asset described as ABYXO. ABYXO is currently presented as an early-stage concept. Unless independently confirmed through verifiable official communications, do not assume a token exists, has launched, or has a deployed contract. This disclosure is not an invitation or recommendation to transact.

1. You may lose everything

Digital assets and meme coins are highly speculative. Their prices can change rapidly or fall to zero. You may lose all funds committed, and there may be no buyer or practical way to exit a position.

2. Volatility, liquidity, and market manipulation

Trading may be thin, fragmented, or unavailable. Prices, trading volume, market capitalization, and liquidity figures shown by third-party sources can be delayed, estimated, incomplete, or calculated differently. Markets may be affected by concentrated ownership, bots, wash trading, pump-and-dump activity, sudden liquidity removal, or other manipulation.

3. Smart contracts and blockchain transactions

Smart contracts, bridges, wallets, decentralized applications, and blockchain infrastructure may contain bugs or vulnerabilities, be exploited, or behave unexpectedly. Transactions are generally irreversible. Sending assets to a wrong address, interacting with a malicious contract, signing a harmful transaction, or losing access to a wallet may result in permanent loss.

4. Wallet, key, and impersonation risks

Anyone who obtains your seed phrase or private key may control your assets. ABYXO will never need your seed phrase or private key. Be alert to fake tokens, cloned websites, counterfeit social accounts, malicious links, fake support agents, airdrop scams, and unsolicited direct messages. Verify every domain and contract address independently.

5. Technology and third-party dependency

Base, RPC providers, wallets, explorers, exchanges, data feeds, Clanker, Supabase, hosting providers, and other third-party services may experience outages, failures, attacks, policy changes, or data inaccuracies. ABYXO does not control these services and cannot guarantee their availability or security.

6. Regulatory, tax, and geographic risk

Rules for digital assets differ between countries and may change. An asset or service may be restricted or unavailable where you live. You are responsible for understanding and complying with applicable laws, sanctions, tax reporting, age restrictions, and platform requirements. Obtain independent legal and tax advice where appropriate.

7. No audit or security assurance

Do not assume that a token, contract, website, wallet connection, or third-party integration has been audited, verified, or endorsed. An audit, if one is ever published, cannot eliminate all risk and should be checked for scope, date, and auditor identity.

8. No reliance on projections or roadmap

Roadmaps, plans, token parameters, community goals, and future features may change, be delayed, or never occur. Past performance, online popularity, rankings, and social media attention are not reliable indicators of future results.

9. Personal suitability

Only make decisions you understand and can afford. Do not use borrowed money, emergency savings, or funds needed for essential expenses to speculate. Consider consulting an independent qualified financial professional. ABYXO does not provide individualized investment advice.

10. Acknowledgment

By choosing to interact with digital assets after viewing this website, you acknowledge that you are doing so at your own risk and that you are responsible for independently verifying the relevant information and consequences.

This disclosure summarizes material risks but cannot list every possible risk. It is general information, not financial, legal, or tax advice and not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.
