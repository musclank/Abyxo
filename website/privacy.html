PRIVACY

How information may be handled when you visit the website or use the arcade leaderboard.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This notice describes the information practices for the ABYXO website hosted on GitHub Pages. The project is an early-stage concept. The person or entity legally responsible for operating ABYXO has not been identified on this website; this notice should be updated with verified operator and contact details before any broader public launch.

1. Information you provide

If you choose to create an arcade leaderboard account, the account flow asks for an email address, password, and public username. Authentication is handled by Supabase. ABYXO's leaderboard interface retrieves usernames and game statistics such as best score, best level, and completed runs. Email addresses are not displayed in the public leaderboard. Do not use your real name or sensitive information as your public username.

2. Game and device information

The arcade stores a personal best score in your browser's local storage on that device. This value is used to show your local best score. Clearing browser storage or changing devices may remove or reset it. The website may also receive ordinary technical information as part of hosting, network requests, security, and third-party service operations; the project does not currently describe a separate analytics or advertising profile.

3. Why information is used

  • To create, authenticate, and manage optional leaderboard accounts.
  • To display public usernames and game rankings.
  • To receive and record game score submissions, prevent abuse, and enforce submission limits.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the website and its connected services.

4. Service providers and disclosures

The website uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for authentication and leaderboard database functions, and third-party content delivery or font services for website assets. The Top Clankers page retrieves market information through a Supabase Edge Function that connects to Clanker public data services. These providers may process technical request data under their own terms and privacy notices. The project does not control their independent processing.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect the website and users from abuse or security incidents, or in connection with a legitimate project restructuring. The website does not intentionally publish account email addresses in leaderboard results.

5. Public leaderboard

Your selected username and game statistics are intended to be visible to other visitors when you participate in the global leaderboard. Do not submit personal, confidential, offensive, or identifying information as a username. A public score may remain visible until it is changed or removed from the service.

6. Security and retention

Reasonable technical safeguards are used through the selected hosting and database services, but no internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Account and leaderboard information may be retained while the account and service remain active, and for a reasonable period in backups, security records, or where retention is legally required. Exact retention periods depend in part on the service providers and project configuration.

7. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to create an account and can use the informational parts of the website without joining the leaderboard. You can clear your local browser storage to remove the locally stored arcade best score. For requests to access, correct, or delete leaderboard account information, use a verified official ABYXO contact channel once one is published. Until an official contact channel is listed, do not send passwords, private keys, or other secrets through social media or direct messages.

8. Children and eligibility

The website and leaderboard are not designed for children. Do not create an account if you are below the age required to use the service under the laws that apply to you. The project does not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information.

9. International processing and legal rights

Hosting and service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Depending on your location, you may have privacy rights under applicable law. The availability and scope of those rights depend on the law and the identity and location of the responsible operator.

10. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when features, providers, or legal requirements change. The website currently does not publish a verified privacy contact address or named data controller. Those details should be added before public launch so privacy requests can be directed to the responsible operator.

This is a transparent preliminary privacy notice based on the website features currently present. It is not a certification of compliance with every privacy law. The project operator should confirm data flows, Supabase settings, retention, and applicable legal requirements before launch.
