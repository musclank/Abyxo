01 / THE ORIGIN STORY

THE DEEP
WAS NEVER
THIS LOUD

Long before the memes reached the surface, a strange little signal pulsed through the deepest trenches of the internet. It travelled past forgotten wallets, abandoned group chats, and the remains of old trends.

Something answered. A small, dark-blue octopus emerged from the digital deep, eyes glowing cyan and a golden crown sitting proudly on its head. Nobody knows where the crown came from. ABYXO refuses to comment.

Now the creature has surfaced on Base, carrying one mission: turn the quietest corner of the abyss into the loudest place on the timeline.

Cute face. Suspicious plans. Eight ways to cause trouble.
