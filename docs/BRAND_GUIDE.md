# ABYXO Brand Guide

## Identity
- Name: ABYXO
- Tagline: Small Creature. Deep Chaos.
- Community: Abyxonauts
- Theme: mysterious deep sea meets playful internet culture on Base.

## Mascot
A cute-but-dangerous small dark-blue octopus with glowing cyan eyes, a mischievous expression, and a permanent gold crown.
